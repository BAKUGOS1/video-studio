# Cinematic and product direction

## Story and continuity
Turn the brief into a short progression: situation, change, payoff. Do not force three acts onto a six-second clip. A shot earns its place by revealing something, directing attention, or advancing the story.
Track subject appearance, props, lighting direction, geography, palette, and first/last frame continuity across generated shots. Prefer purposeful cuts over disconnected attractive clips.

## Product films
Capture accurate pages at a useful resolution; mask private information. Show the one interaction that proves the promise. Choose a focal point per shot. Use macro crops to make interface details readable, restrained camera movement to reveal hierarchy, and a final stable frame for the takeaway.
Optional Shotcraft techniques include page camera moves, progressive UI assembly, typography reveals, and beat-aligned cuts. Retrieve the actual upstream recipe before claiming to use a named card. Avoid imposing an upstream template's brand on the user's product.

## Shot prompts
Specify:
- Subject and concrete action.
- Setting, time, and spatial relationship.
- Shot size, angle, lens feel, depth of field.
- One coherent camera movement.
- Light direction, softness, practical sources.
- Palette, texture, emotional tone.
- Continuity anchors and desired end state.
Keep duration/resolution/aspect ratio as API parameters where supported. Do not cram incompatible camera moves into a short generation. Distinguish desired controls from controls supported by the inspected schema.

## Sound and pacing
Make speech timing lead the edit for narrated videos. Use beat cues sparingly for music-led promos. Allow pauses around important messages. Use a restrained sound palette with clean fades and no clipped peaks. Check the last frame and ending audio tail deliberately.

## Brand
Use supplied brand tokens, logo clear space, fonts, and motion rules. Read applicable project instructions and brand/story documentation. Apply rules according to the requested surface; do not transfer project-specific restrictions to unrelated work.
